Introduction

I am an aspiring astrophysicist and researcher driven by a fundamental question: How did our universe evolve, and what secrets are hidden within its most extreme environments? As a BS-MS student at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Pune, I bridge theoretical physics with computational modeling to study complex cosmic phenomena.

My core academic and research interests lie at the intersection of Cosmology, High-Energy Astrophysics, Dark Matter, and Black Hole physics. Whether parsing large astronomical datasets or auditing advanced theoretical frameworks, I am passionate about decoding the mathematical laws that govern the universe.
